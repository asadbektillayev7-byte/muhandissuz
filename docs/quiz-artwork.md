# Quiz illustration assets

Created for the quiz catalogue with the built-in image-generation tool. The visual direction is dimensional engineering objects with a consistent purple, gold, and turquoise palette, complemented by the existing Murvachcha mascot. No Brilliant or Blooket artwork is copied into the site.

Final assets: `public/images/quiz/{ai,mechanical,electrical,civil,chemical,environmental,motorsports,general}.webp`. Each is 512 × 512 with alpha transparency, encoded at WebP quality 86. These are optional fallback covers; uploaded quiz covers retain priority. Files are loaded individually so a visitor only downloads topics displayed on the page.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single production illustration sprite atlas for an engineering quiz website for teenagers ages 12–17. Create ONE transparent PNG atlas, landscape 1536 x 1024, arranged in an exact 4-column by 2-row grid of eight equal 384 x 512 cells. No visible grid or backgrounds. Every object centered in its cell with generous transparent margin, fully inside its own cell; consistent visual scale. Original vivid dimensional educational illustrations: polished chunky 3D forms, crisp silhouette, rounded bevels, satin material, subtle self-shadowing, rich purple and electric indigo balanced with golden yellow and turquoise, high readability at small sizes. Friendly and curious, sophisticated teen-friendly rather than toddler toys. Top row, left to right: (1) AI: a charming purple desktop robot head with two small turquoise eyes, a tiny smile, golden antenna, with a small yellow processor block beside it; (2) mechanical engineering: two substantial interlocking yellow and purple gears with a small turquoise wrench, strong dimensional teeth; (3) electrical engineering: a yellow articulated desk lamp glowing softly over a purple circuit board with turquoise traces; (4) civil engineering: a miniature elegant golden suspension bridge on two violet foundations. Bottom row, left to right: (5) chemistry: a purple laboratory flask with bright turquoise liquid and three golden bubbles, beside one small yellow molecular model; (6) environmental engineering: a compact turquoise wind turbine and a vivid green leaf on a violet platform; (7) motorsports: a dynamic miniature purple and golden yellow open-wheel racing car in three-quarter view with turquoise wheel hubs; (8) general engineering: an open violet toolbox holding a golden wrench, turquoise screwdriver and yellow lightbulb. Consistent elevated three-quarter view, soft studio lighting from upper left. Objects only, alpha-transparent background, no ground planes, no text, no lettering, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no characters beyond the AI robot face. The atlas must keep all eight cell contents completely separate for exact programmatic slicing. Do not reproduce any existing brand artwork.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this same transparent engineering illustration atlas for production. Keep the eight objects, exact 4-column by 2-row grid, 1536x1024 canvas, same order and colours and styling. Important change: reduce EACH object group to about 70% of its current size and center it in its own 384x512 cell, leaving at least 45 pixels of fully transparent padding on every side of each cell. No part of an object may cross a cell boundary. Especially fit the chemistry molecule and racing car completely within their own cells. Remove all background glows and atmosphere; retain only the solid object silhouettes and their own internal shading. True transparent alpha background everywhere between objects, no cast shadows or floor, no text or labels. Make the surfaces slightly softer satin rather than chrome/gloss. This must be a clean usable sprite atlas that can be sliced at x=384,768,1152 and y=512 with zero cut-off edges.

The final atlas was sliced around each complete object, trimmed, and fitted to a transparent square. Murvachcha reuses `public/images/murvachcha-sprites.webp`; his artwork and shared assistant behavior are unchanged.
